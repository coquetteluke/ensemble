ENSEMBLE: FIRST LAUNCH

1. Double-click Install Ensemble in this window.

2. macOS shows: "Install Ensemble" Not Opened. Press Done, not Move to Trash.

   This is expected. The message means Apple has not checked this app. It does
   not mean the app is harmful. Checking (notarization) needs a paid Apple
   Developer account, and this project does not have one.

3. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down to Security.
   Next to "Install Ensemble" was blocked to protect your Mac, press Open Anyway.
   Press Open Anyway again in the dialog, then use Touch ID or your Mac password.

4. The installer opens. Press Install, then Install the engine. It copies
   Ensemble to your Applications folder, downloads the engine (OpenCode) for
   you, and opens Ensemble. You do not need Homebrew. macOS does not ask again
   for Ensemble itself.

To skip steps 2 and 3, eject this disk, run this in Terminal, and open the
download again:

       xattr -d com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/Ensemble-*.dmg

This removes the "downloaded from the internet" mark from the download only.
It does not change any setting on your Mac.

Download Ensemble only from the project's own page, and compare the SHA-256
of the disk image with the one published there before you open it:

       shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/Ensemble-*.dmg

The full guide, with pictures, is docs/INSTALL.md in the Ensemble repository.
